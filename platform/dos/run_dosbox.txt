Inside DOSBox (adjust the host path):

    mount c C:\path\to\traffic-signal\platform\dos
    c:
    build.bat                  (MASM 6.x: ml + link; ML.EXE and LINK.EXE must be on the DOS PATH)
    traffic                    (10 / 3 / 10 s)
    traffic 15 4 12            (green yellow red, each 1..99 s)

TASM instead of MASM:  build_tasm.bat
Cross-assemble on Linux/macOS with UASM:  ./build_uasm.sh
